METADATA: Great Wall of China, History, Defense, Ming Dynasty

[ENGLISH SECTION]
The Great Wall of China is a series of fortifications that were built across the historical northern borders of ancient Chinese states and Imperial China as protection against various nomadic groups. The best-known sections of the wall were built by the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644). It stretches over 21,000 kilometers in total.
