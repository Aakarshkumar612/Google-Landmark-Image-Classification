METADATA: Eiffel Tower, Paris, France, Gustave Eiffel, Iron Lattice

[ENGLISH SECTION]
The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It is named after the engineer Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built the tower in 1889. It was originally constructed as the entrance arch to the 1889 World's Fair and is now a global cultural icon of France.
